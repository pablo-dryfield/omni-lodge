# Partners Portal

## Scope and ownership

Channels are the accounting/reporting parent. One optional partner profile belongs to one existing Channel; enabling a partner must not rename a Channel or change historical bookings. Company members are ordinary login users without a staff profile/badge requirement. Every operation resolves active company membership or company-wide administrator/owner/manager authorization server-side. Company settings and commercial contracts are business records; operational switches/limits use the existing Control Panel.

The release covers hosted links/QR, an embeddable booking form, direct portal reservations/payment links, partner API, referral vouchers, group requests, packages of approved OmniLodge products, and validated bulk imports. Existing supplier products sold together with ours require a supplier inventory connector; the system must not pretend to reserve inventory it does not control. Third-party booking software can integrate with the documented API; vendor-specific connectors and OCTO certification require actual vendor interoperability testing and are not implied by an API endpoint.

## Booking engine

All entry points use server-calculated storefront quotes, availability/weekday/add-on rules, resource locking/reservations and order-to-booking projection. Product access is restricted to the partner agreement. Public forms only collect through OmniLodge; they cannot assert that a partner received money. Partner-collected and pay-at-arrival reservations require agreement permission. Partner-collected customer payment is recorded separately from the partner's net debt to OmniLodge. Requests do not reserve availability until approved. Packages create separate operational booking lines for each experience. A bulk import reports each row's outcome and uses stable references on retries.

Every submission has a partner-scoped unique reference plus a canonical request fingerprint. A replay returns the same order; changed payload with the same reference returns a conflict. SQL transactions/row locks protect concurrent retries and credit checks. Orders and commissions retain immutable agreement snapshots. Customer/booking data is never returned from a public referral code. Public pages show a generic payment-return message only; booking status and customer details require authenticated company access. Company access is checked on every read/write, including exports, statements, tokens and webhook events.

## Payments and settlements

Default: customer pays the platform through Stripe-hosted Checkout. Partner-collected bookings and approved arrival payment are separate modes. Stripe Connect onboarding links an Express connected account; no partner secret Stripe key is requested or embedded. Test/live account associations are separate. Transfers use the platform merchant that took payment, a verified succeeded charge, server-calculated commission and provider idempotency. Percentage commissions reduce proportionally after refunds; fixed commissions persist until booked units change or cancellation. Unavailable balance/provider failures remain actionable reconciliation items and are retried; they must not be marked settled. The platform retains Stripe fees/refund/dispute liability under separate charges and transfers. A charge dispute freezes/reverses partner allocation until resolved. Only successful provider responses change transfer records. Existing staff affiliate rules are not reused as company contracts.

Account onboarding/KYC, supported country/currency and platform Connect activation are external activation requirements. They must be displayed clearly; local tests never transfer real money. Off-platform settlement records require a reference and an actor/reason audit trail and do not themselves move bank funds. Do not automatically modify the existing finance ledger without a verified transaction/account mapping.

Partial refunds have their own agreement permission and API scope. The original platform Stripe payment is verified and its remaining refundable balance enforced. A stable operation reference bound to the immutable Stripe payment identity, plus provider-history lookup, prevents duplicate refunds after retries or a database rollback. Transfer and reversal keys also use the immutable provider payment identity instead of regenerable ledger row IDs. Refunds update operational financial snapshots, rule-based earned commission and transfer adjustments. Partner-collected refunds require verified external receipt evidence; recording evidence never initiates a bank transfer. Monetary refunds alone do not remove attendees or extras. The owner selected automatic price adjustments. Amendments can change products, dates/times, guest counts and extras. Every change has a unique operation reference and an accepted quote. Stripe price increases generate hosted Checkout for the difference; the original booking stays in place until payment is verified and the revised inventory can be committed. Price decreases automatically refund the difference up to the actual remaining payment. An unavailable paid upgrade refunds its extra payment and preserves the original booking. A separate immutable payment ledger retains every original/additional charge and transfer. Price-adjustment refunds are distinguished from later monetary refunds to avoid deducting them twice in booking reports. Unpaid arrival reservations simply update the amount due. Payments originally collected outside the platform cannot be refunded through its Stripe merchant; operations must verify the collector's adjustment receipt. Amendment payment links expire independently of the original reservation and can be cancelled. Signed provider events and the repair worker complete/retry pending adjustments.

## Integrations and security

Opaque public sales codes carry attribution, not payment or management authority. API bearer tokens are random, hashed, shown once, scoped, expiring and revocable. Separate sandbox behavior uses the application's non-production database and Stripe test merchant. Never return token hashes or webhook secrets in ordinary responses. Webhook events are persisted with stable IDs, signed over timestamp plus exact JSON bytes, and delivered with bounded retries. HTTPS destinations must pass DNS/IP public-network validation on every connection; redirects are disabled. Delivery history and replay are company-scoped. A background worker also reconciles paid orders, transfer reversals and missed notifications. Local outbound-email/background-job guards stay enabled.

The dedicated embedded form uses an explicit partner domain allowlist in frame-ancestors; checkout navigates to a Stripe-hosted page in the top-level browsing context. The internal OmniLodge UI is not framed. No customer names or credentials appear in URLs. Rate limits, payload/row limits and configurable feature flags are enforced server-side.

## User experience

Responsive Partners page: company switcher; overview; bookings with search/status/payment filters and pagination; new booking with multiple experience lines; group requests with approval; packages/vouchers; team; integrations with copy/QR/embed/API documentation and delivery log; statements with explicit earned/transferred/owed/refunded amounts. Company-wide managers configure the Channel, approved products, commission, payment modes, credit ceiling, cancellation cutoff and permitted domains. Company members have owner/agent/developer roles with least privilege. Money labels distinguish customer payment, partner commission and net amount owed.

## Flexible commissions

Agreements support fixed PLN amounts and percentages per guest place, distinct experience departure, or booking, plus one override for each approved product. Default rules apply only to products without overrides. A per-booking product override runs once across that product's departures; the default per-booking rule runs once across the other products. Duplicate lines do not create extra experience units, while different departure times do. Integer discount allocation matches operational booking projection. Uniform percentages are not multiplied by guest counts. The initial quote rejects commission above customer revenue.

Each new PartnerBooking stores a versioned commission calculation and immutable agreement snapshot. Amendments recalculate booked units using that agreement, including zero-price-difference guest/product changes. Money-only refunds retain fixed fees and scale percentages; full cancellation/dispute earns zero. Refunds can leave fixed fees greater than retained revenue, which is an explicit platform liability. The operational refund projection is bounded by the current selection price without losing original payment/refund history. Existing rows without a calculation retain legacy reconciliation and transfer identities; no historical agreement or payout is rewritten.

New-rule Stripe allocations use complete paginated provider history grouped by immutable payment intent, verify charge/currency/amount/metadata, and reconcile the aggregate earned target across original and amendment receipts. SQL row locks serialize operations. Provider-state-based transfer/reversal keys recover success after a SQL rollback without another payment. Top-ups use a verified source charge when permitted; later catch-up or refund-funded fixed fees may require platform balance. Failure preserves customer refund evidence, observes actual transferred money where possible, and leaves a visible reconciliation error rather than inventing a settled balance. A contract with no automatic split does not query transfer history unnecessarily.

Partner-collected statements retain separate cumulative incoming settlement receipts and outgoing credit payments, avoiding negative incoming ledgers. Refund credits can be recorded with a unique verified external receipt and actor/reason; duplicate or excessive receipts are rejected. A later cancellation can recover a credit already paid. Public sales quotes exclude the private commission breakdown; authenticated partner quotes include it. Commercial rates remain audited partner agreement records, not global runtime configuration.
